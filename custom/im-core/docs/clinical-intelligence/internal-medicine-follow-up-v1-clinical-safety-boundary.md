## 2. Purpose

This document defines the Clinical Safety Boundary for future Clinical Intelligence capabilities in the Internal Medicine Follow-Up V1 module of OpenEMR IM Core.

The purpose is to clearly define what future Clinical Intelligence may support, what it must not decide, what requires clinician review, what must remain non-autonomous, and what claims are prohibited.

This document protects the project from unsafe interpretation, unsafe implementation, unsupported marketing claims, premature clinical-use claims, and unintended movement toward autonomous clinical decision-making.

This document does not implement clinical intelligence, runtime rules, SQL, AI, predictive analytics, OpenEMR runtime changes, OpenEMR core changes, or production clinical decision support.
