## 2. Purpose

This document defines the documentation-only Trend Review Plan for future Clinical Intelligence support in the Internal Medicine Follow-Up V1 module.

The purpose is to define safe, clinician-reviewed, source-traceable boundaries for future longitudinal review across encounters.

Trend review may later support clinicians by organizing documented changes over time, including vital signs, chronic disease status, medication changes, symptoms, assessment changes, plan changes, open loops, and safety-net follow-up.

This document does not implement trend review, runtime rules, SQL, machine learning, predictive analytics, autonomous clinical decision support, or production clinical decision support.
